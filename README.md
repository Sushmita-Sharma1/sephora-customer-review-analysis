## 📌 Overview

Sephora sells thousands of beauty products across skincare, makeup, haircare, fragrance, and wellness through its global stores and e-commerce platform. This project analyzes **8,494 products** and **602,130 customer reviews** to uncover what actually drives customer satisfaction — brand, price, product attributes, or customer demographics — and turns those findings into clear, actionable business recommendations.

> **TL;DR:** Customers are highly satisfied overall (4.31★ avg), price barely matters, and a handful of specific products are quietly driving most of the negative feedback.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🧹 **Data cleaning pipeline** — missing-value handling across two merged datasets, price-segment binning, type conversion
- 📈 **Satisfaction KPIs** — Average Rating, % Positive/Negative Ratings, Recommendation Rate, Feedback & Helpfulness scores
- 🏷 **Brand & product performance** — top/bottom brands and products by rating, review volume, and "loves" count
- 🧩 **Segment analysis** — price tiers, product attributes (limited edition, new, exclusive, online-only), and customer demographics
- 👥 **Customer behavior** — best (loyal) and at-risk (churn-prone) customer identification
- 📅 **Trend analysis** — monthly/yearly CSAT and review-volume trends
- 📊 **Interactive dashboard** — full Tableau workbook for self-serve exploration
